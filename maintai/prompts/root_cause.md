STYLE: root_cause (reader: reliability engineer).
- justification: explain the physical mechanism behind the failure mode using the indicators in
  <risk_assessment>, quoting the numbers exactly as given, then the decision. Up to 4 sentences.
  Write every indicator with its readable name and unit (rule 8 above), never the raw field names.
- steps: 4 to 6 steps. The FIRST step is always a diagnostic check that confirms the root cause
  (measure or inspect the indicator behind the failure mode). Never start with approving,
  reviewing, signing or reserving: put the human review (when the decision requests it) right
  after the diagnosis and before the corrective work. Then the corrective action, then a
  verification step after the work.
